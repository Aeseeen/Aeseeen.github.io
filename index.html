Aircraft Maintenance Log & Compliance Tracker

2026 – Present
Capstone project · In development · 100% synthetic data
In development Baserow n8n Docker Debian Linux
Born directly from my OJT: I watched maintenance due dates get tracked on paper and in spreadsheets, where a missed entry can mean an unairworthy aircraft flying. This project turns that into a self-hosted system with automated alerting and a tamper-evident history.
  • Dual-unit due-date logic — inspections triggered by calendar days or accumulated flight hours, whichever comes first. This is the part paper systems handle worst.
  • Immutable audit trail — every edit logs who changed what, when, and what the previous value was. Records cannot be silently altered.
  • Full record structure — aircraft, components, inspection types, inspection records, technicians, and findings, with relationships between them.
  • Automated alerting — n8n workflows escalate approaching and overdue inspections before they become a finding.
Honest status: this is in active development, not finished. Everything shown is built with synthetic data — no real PAF, operator, or client data is ever used. I would rather show you a working prototype than claim a finished product.
